Clairvoyant · logo film · keyframe storyboard

Orb chases the spark

Orb's antenna spark pops off and flies away, drawing a rising line chart. Orb chases it up the bars. At the top, the spark is out of reach, so Orb takes a leap of faith and catches it. The spark doesn't stop: it yanks him round in a full loop over the chart, and the loop they draw becomes the logo's crystal-ball C. They slingshot out through the gap, Orb lands on the groundline, and the title comes in.

The idea

Why this climax works

The chase draws the logo

The rising line is the chart inside the ball. The loop Orb is dragged through is the C. The gap is where they burst out. No morph or magic transition is needed: the action leaves the mark behind.

Orb earns it

He falls short once, then psychs himself up and jumps into empty space. The climax is a choice he makes, which suits a mascot who is curious, a bit gormless and brave.

The payoff is fun

Catching the spark isn't the end; it's the start of the ride. The loop-the-loop is the biggest, silliest moment, and it happens right before the title, so the film ends on energy.

If the loop doesn't land

Two other endings

Both reuse panels 1–7 and only change what happens after the leap.

Through his eye

Orb misses the catch and drops back onto the top bar, defeated. He looks up, and the camera pushes into his eye. Reflected in it is the whole chase, curved by the lens into the C, with the spark at the gap. Pull out: his eye has become the logo.

Quieter and more clever. Puts "clairvoyant" in his eye. Loses the big action beat.

He holds it up

Orb catches the spark but falls with it, and lands under the chart. He braces and pushes the whole chart up over his head. His legs splay into the V and his feet into the groundline. Then he steps out and the mark stays standing.

Funny and heroic, and explains the V stand. Puts him very close to the mark, so the step-out must be clean.

Hand-off

Notes for the animator

Physics

  • Hops are parabolas at constant horizontal speed, with a crouch before and a squash after.
  • In the loop the spark leads and Orb trails on a stretched antenna. Stretch the stalk most at the bottom of the loop, where the pull is strongest.
  • Orb's body spins with the loop so the antenna always points along the path.

Colour logic

  • Amber is the data: the spark, the rising line, the breakout. The loop trail is ice white, because it becomes the white C.
  • The chart line only goes up. Bars shift from data-blue to brand white as the ring settles; the last bar stays amber.
  • End on the official lockup, untouched. Orb leaves the frame before the wordmark lands.

Sound

  • A marimba note per bar landing, climbing the scale.
  • Leap: everything drops out but a breath. Catch: a bright "click".
  • Loop: a rising whoosh with Orb's "wheee" at the top; the slingshot is a rubber-band twang. Title: one warm chord.
