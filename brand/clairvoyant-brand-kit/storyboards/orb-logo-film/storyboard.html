Orb chases the spark

Orb's antenna spark pops off and flies away, drawing a rising line chart. Orb chases it up the bars. At the top, the spark hovers just past the tip of the line, out of reach. Orb takes a leap of faith, catches it, and merges into the tip: he becomes the logo's breakout point. The camera pulls back, the C draws round the chart, the stand rises, and the title comes in.

The idea

Why this climax works

The chase draws the logo

The rising line is the chart inside the ball, and the spark's trail is the breakout line. When Orb merges into the tip, he becomes the one point the whole logo is pointing at.

Orb earns it

He falls short once, then psychs himself up and jumps into empty space. The climax is a choice he makes, which suits a mascot who is curious, a bit gormless and brave.

The payoff is the mark

Catching the spark completes him, and merging puts him exactly where the data is going. Everything after that is the logo assembling around him: the C, the stand, the wordmark.

If the merge doesn't land

Two other endings

Both reuse panels 1–7 and only change what happens after the leap.

Through his eye

Orb misses the catch and drops back onto the top bar, defeated. He looks up, and the camera pushes into his eye. Reflected in it is the whole chase, curved by the lens into the C, with the spark at the gap. Pull out: his eye has become the logo.

Quieter and more clever. Puts "clairvoyant" in his eye. Loses the big action beat.

The loop

The spark doesn't stop when he catches it. It drags him round a full loop over the chart, and their trail becomes the C. He's flung out of the gap and lands beside the stand.

The biggest, silliest action beat. Orb survives the film, but the C is drawn by him rather than becoming him.

Hand-off

Notes for the animator

Physics

  • Hops are parabolas at constant horizontal speed, with a crouch before and a squash after.
  • The leap ends with his antenna tip exactly on the spark, so the catch needs no snap or cheat.
  • In the merge his body centre slides into the point as he shrinks; his feet never leave the air.

Colour logic

  • Amber is the data: the spark, the rising line, the breakout. Orb turns white as he merges, then the point flashes amber.
  • The chart line only goes up. Bars shift from data-blue to brand white as the ring settles; the last bar stays amber.
  • End on the official lockup, untouched. Orb is gone by then: he is the point.

Sound

  • A marimba note per bar landing, climbing the scale.
  • Leap: everything drops out but a breath. Catch: a bright "click".
  • Merge: a soft rising "shoop" into a warm ping. Reveal: a big riser. Title: one warm chord.
