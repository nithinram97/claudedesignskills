Orb chases the spark

Orb's antenna spark pops off and draws a rising line chart. Orb chases it up the bars, which are the logo's four bars at film scale. At the amber bar the spark climbs out of reach. Orb stops chasing, sees where the line is going, gets there first and waits. The spark snaps back on, the camera pulls back to show the whole scene was the logo, and Orb melts into the breakout point.

"We don't chase the number. We see where it's going."

Brand kit rules

Checked against the kit

  • The line only goes up. Every trail point is higher than the last. The old 2D pass's dip-bar stumble is replaced by a teeter on the amber bar's edge.
  • No decorative circles. There are no shockwave or target rings. The only circle is the C ring, which draws in at the reveal.
  • Jumps are physical. Every hop is a parabola at constant horizontal speed, with a crouch before it and a squash on landing.
  • The chart is the logo. Bars, trail and breakout use the mark's exact geometry, so the reveal is a camera pull-back, not a morph.
  • Orb never clips the bars. He lands with both feet on the bar tops, at least a foot-width in from the edges, except on purpose for the teeter.
  • Celebrate big. The catch has happy eyes, a grin, glowing cheeks, sparkles and a spinning jump.

Notes for the animator

Climax

  • The turn is Orb choosing to stop. Hold the teeter and the failed jump long enough that the audience wants him to give up.
  • Foresight dashes are ice (#CFE3F5), not amber. Amber belongs to the real data and the spark.
  • After he waits at the point, give the spark 3–4 frames of slow-in so the snap reads as the spark coming to him.

Reveal and title

  • Pull back on one continuous ease-out. The bars must land exactly on the mark's bar rectangles.
  • The C ring draws from the top end of the gap, over the top and round to the bottom end, so the gap stays open where the breakout passes through.
  • Bars shift from data-blue to brand white as the ring closes. The amber bar stays amber.
  • End on the official stacked lockup, dark variant, untouched. Orb is not in the lockup.

Sound

  • A marimba note per bar landing, climbing the scale.
  • On the failed jump, a falling "wah" for comedy; the chart still never dips.
  • Foresight: everything ducks, one sustained shimmer. Catch: 4 frames of silence, then a bright chord.
